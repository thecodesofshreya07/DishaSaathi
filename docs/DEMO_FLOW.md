# DishaSaathi — Hackathon Demo Flow

## The Story

Government information is not necessarily unavailable.

The problem is that it is fragmented.

A citizen may need to visit multiple departments, portals, forms and documents just to understand what needs to happen first.

DishaSaathi turns that fragmented information into one connected journey.

---

## Demo

### 1. Landing Page

Show:

> Government processes shouldn't feel like a maze.

User enters:

> I want to start a small bakery in Mumbai.

Click:

**Build My Roadmap →**

---

### 2. AI Understanding

Show a short processing state:

Understanding your goal...

Identifying location...

Finding relevant procedures...

Mapping dependencies...

Then show:

**Food Business — Mumbai**

---

### 3. Clarification

Ask only 1–2 useful questions if needed.

Do not make the user fill a long form.

---

### 4. Roadmap

Show the generated journey as an interactive graph.

Example:

Start
↓
Eligibility
↓
Business Registration
↓
Shop Registration
↓
FSSAI
↓
Municipal Permission
↓
Applicable Tax Registration
↓
Complete

Some branches should be parallel where appropriate.

---

### 5. Open a Node

Click:

**FSSAI Registration**

Show the right-side detail panel.

Display:

* Why
* Documents
* Prerequisites
* Authority
* Official source
* Verification status
* Last verified
* Mark Complete

---

### 6. Demonstrate Dependencies

Click a later step.

Example:

GST / applicable registration

Show:

🔒 STEP BLOCKED

Complete the prerequisite first.

Then click:

**Show prerequisite**

The graph highlights the dependency.

This demonstrates that DishaSaathi is more than a checklist.

---

### 7. Demonstrate Source Trust

Open:

**Official Source**

Show:

Source:
Official government portal

Status:
✓ Verified

Last checked:
[actual dataset date]

The source should be accessible.

---

### 8. Demonstrate Change Detection

Open the admin console.

Show a controlled source update:

BEFORE

3 required documents

AFTER

4 required documents

Added:

* Photograph

Click:

**Approve Change**

---

### 9. Roadmap Impact

Return to the citizen journey.

The affected node now shows:

⚠ Requirement updated

Click:

**What Changed?**

Show:

* Photograph

Source:
Official source

The roadmap has updated.

---

### 10. Closing

End with:

> DishaSaathi doesn't replace government portals.
>
> It makes the journey across them understandable.

Then:

**One goal.
One verified journey.
Every dependency explained.**
